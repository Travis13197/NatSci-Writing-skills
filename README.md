# NatSci-Writing-skills  |  A production system for Nature/Science-level papers and figures

**Author:** Chuanyang Liu  |  作者：Chuanyang Liu  
**Citation:** a related paper is in preparation and will be updated; if you use these skills, please cite it and this repository. 

You have data, figures and a claim. The manuscript still needs an argument that survives review, evidence placed where readers can find it, figures that carry the paper, a journal format that matches the target, and responses that hold under scrutiny. This package organizes those jobs into one production system for Nature/Science-level work in life science, biotechnology, bioinformatics and AI-for-science.  |  NatSci-Writing-skills版帮你把原始数据，idea直接组织成一份 Nature/Science 的投稿级别Manuscript。

It is built for complete deliverables: a full manuscript, a full figure set, a complete Perspective, a revision package or a submission-ready file.  |  它面向完整交付物：整篇论文、整套图件、完整 Perspective、返修包，或可直接投稿的文件。

## Who it is for  |  这个skill适合谁？

Use it when you are drafting or rebuilding a manuscript, preparing figures, writing a Nature-style Perspective, revising under review, or converting a draft into a journal-ready package. You bring the science, data and decisions; the package supplies the architecture, checks and production workflow.  |  写新的Manuscript、润色重写旧稿、准备结果图、写 Nature 风格 Perspective、返修，或把Manuscript变成可投稿文件时使用。科学、数据和决定由你提供，包提供结构、检查和成稿流程。

It is made for research groups in life science, biotechnology, bioinformatics and AI-for-science, and for authors who want a full draft with the reasoning kept in the files.  |  面向生命科学、生物技术、生物信息学和 AI-for-science 的研究组，也面向需要完整草稿、并希望保留推理过程的作者。

## What you get  |  你从中能够得到什么？

Two self-contained agent skills, one English and one Chinese. Each has an operating manual, a reference library, working templates and tested Python/R figure starters.  |  中英文各一个自包含技能，每个都带操作手册、参考库、工作模板和已测试的 Python/R 绘图脚本。

Everything runs from the folder: references, templates and scripts are local, links are relative, and the package works offline. Copy the folder, load SKILL.md, and work.  |  所有内容都在文件夹里：参考、模板和脚本都在本地，链接使用相对路径，不需要联网或外部语料。复制文件夹、加载 SKILL.md 就能用。

## What it can do  |  Skill能做什么

### Draft  |  起草

- **Write a complete manuscript.** Title, abstract, introduction, results, discussion, methods, references, legends, statements, supplementary list and change log, in one pass.  |  一次交付整篇：标题、摘要、引言、结果、讨论、方法、参考文献、图注、声明和补充材料清单。
- **Build a submission-grade figure set.** Start from one claim, set the figure contract and panel map, then produce publication styling, statistical display, SVG/PDF/600-dpi files and captions.  |  从一句结论出发，先定图件合同和面板地图，再出投稿级样式、统计呈现、SVG/PDF/600 dpi 文件和图注。
- **Produce a Nature Perspective.** Six-section architecture, evidence ledger, full paragraphs, key-sentence bolding, three conceptual figures plus a box, a bilingual manuscript and a journal-formatted package.  |  按六节结构、证据台账、整段正文、关键句加粗、三幅概念图加一个 Box、中英双语和期刊格式完成整篇 Perspective。
- **Develop a theory or hypothesis paper.** Turn a conceptual claim into definitions, a testable relation, figures and a boundary paragraph.  |  把概念或者hypothesis写成定义、科学严谨的文字、符合要求的结果图。

### Audit  |  审查

- **Judge editorial fit.** Test originality, importance, broad interest, technical soundness and readability before submission, then answer why this, why now and why readers outside the field should care.  |  投稿前检查原创性、重要性、跨领域兴趣、技术可靠性和可读性，再回答为什么做这个、为什么现在做、为什么领域外也关心。
- **Audit statistics and integrity.** Independent unit, replication, exact n and P values, Reporting Summary, image integrity, data/code/materials statements.  |  检查独立实验单位、重复、精确 n 和 P、Reporting Summary、图像诚信、数据/代码/材料声明。
- **Control information density.** Decide what belongs in the main text, captions and SI; keep revisions from making the paper longer and looser.  |  决定什么进正文、图注和补充材料；返修时控制篇幅，让信息更集中。
- **Search and cite properly.** Build a documented search, screen with a PRISMA-style record, verify every reference and manage RIS/BibTeX/Zotero.  |  写检索计划，按 PRISMA 式流程筛选，逐条核对文献，管理 RIS/BibTeX/Zotero。

### Revise and submit  |  修改与投稿

- **Revise and polish.** Fix the argument, then the sentences; remove AI tone and translationese; mark changes in English and Chinese.  |  修改润色论证语言；去掉 AI 腔和翻译腔；中英对照标出改动。
- **Edit with author-text fidelity.** Light polish, author-faithful expansion, translation or tracked revision; preserve claim order and wording, mark changes and keep the source untouched.  |  做轻度润色、基于作者语言进行忠实扩写、翻译或修订模式；保留断言顺序和措辞，标出改动，源文件保持不动。
- **Format for a target journal.** Nature, Science or Nature Communications structure, references, statements, equations and line numbers, with the science unchanged.  |  按目标期刊调整结构、参考文献、声明、公式和行号，科学内容保持原样。
- **Respond to reviewers.** Point-by-point letter, comment tracker, action mapping, tone control and change checklist.  |  逐条回复、意见跟踪表、动作映射、语气控制和修改清单。
- **Comply with AI policy.** Disclose as the journal requires, check prohibited uses, verify AI-suggested references and numbers, and remove AI tone.  |  按期刊要求披露，检查禁止用途，核实 AI 提到的文献和数字，清掉 AI 腔。

## What makes it different  |  它和普通写作提示词有什么不同吗？

- **Argument before prose.** Every task starts from the claim, the evidence chain and the figure-level conclusion. Sentences come after those decisions.  |  先定主张、证据链和图级结论，再动句子。
- **An evidence ledger.** Claims carry their status: established, definitional, model-dependent, hypothesis or prospective experiment.  |  每条断言标明状态：已确立、定义性、模型依赖、假说或前瞻实验。
- **Full paragraphs.** Manuscript prose uses connected paragraphs. Lists stay outside the argument, and key insights get one bold sentence at a time.  |  正文用连贯段落；列表留在论证之外；洞见用单句加粗。
- **A style gate.** A checker scans for template contrast, defensive language, AI vocabulary and related patterns; CI runs it on the READMEs.  |  文风检查器扫描模板对立、防御性表达和高频 AI 词，CI 会检查 README。
- **Author fidelity.** Light polish preserves claim order and wording; tracked revisions work on a copy and leave the source untouched.  |  轻度润色保留断言顺序和措辞；修订模式在副本上操作，源文件不动。


## How it works  |  背后的做法

Top-journal papers usually stumble in three places: the argument is thin, the evidence sits in the wrong place, or the prose and figures keep a busy reader from seeing why it matters.  |  顶刊稿子常见三类问题：论证单薄、证据放错位置、文字和图让忙碌的读者看不出重点。

The workflow sets four gates: innovation and editorial fit before drafting; main-text discipline and evidence chains while drafting; statistics, reporting integrity and figure audits before submission; point-by-point response and claim calibration after review.  |  流程设四道关口：动笔前查创新和编辑契合；写作中管正文纪律和证据链；投稿前查统计、报告诚信和图件；返修时做逐点回复和断言校准。

Each gate has a concrete output: a canvas, a claim table, a figure contract, a reporting checklist or a response tracker. The point is to make the next decision visible.  |  每道关口都有具体产出：创新画布、断言表、图件合同、报告检查表或回复跟踪表。每一步都让下一个决定变得可见。

## AI use and integrity  |  AI 使用底线

AI can help with language editing, structure, literature triage, formatting, code and figure layout. Authorship belongs to humans, and the authors remain responsible for every claim, citation, number, image and permission.  |  AI 可以辅助语言编辑、结构梳理、文献初筛、格式处理、写代码和调图件布局。署名作者必须是人，每条结论、引文、数字、图像和许可由作者负责。

Keep unpublished, confidential, peer-review and patient material in controlled environments. Data, research images, references, statistics and approvals remain author-verified. Treat AI-suggested references as leads and verify each one.  |  未发表、机密、审稿中的材料和患者数据留在受控环境。数据、研究图像、参考文献、统计和批件都由作者核实。AI 推荐的文献逐条核对。

When the target journal requires disclosure, state the tool, the task, the sections affected and how the output was checked.  |  目标期刊要求披露时，写清工具、任务、涉及章节和核验过程。

Full policy table, disclosure template and audit: references/ai-use-and-writing-integrity.md; record template: assets/templates/ai-disclosure.md. Policies change; check the target journal before submission.  |  完整政策表、披露模板和审计清单见 references/ai-use-and-writing-integrity.md；记录模板见 assets/templates/ai-disclosure.md。政策会变，投稿前按目标期刊核对。

## Quick start  |  快速开始

1. Copy one or both skill folders into your agent's skills directory, together with references/ and assets/.  |  把一个或两个技能目录复制到 agent 的 skills 目录，references/ 和 assets/ 一起带上。
2. Refresh the catalog.  |  刷新技能目录。
3. Ask for the outcome: "write the complete paper" or "build the full figure set".  |  直接说结果，例如"写完整论文""做整套投稿图"。

~~~bash
python tools/install.py --target ~/.agents/skills --skills both --dry-run
python tools/install.py --target ~/.agents/skills --skills both
~~~

Four ways to start:

- "Use natsci-writing-zh. Turn these data and figures into a complete Nature paper and list what is missing."  |  把这些数据和图写成完整 Nature 论文，缺什么列出来。
- "Use natsci-writing-zh. Build a submission-grade figure set for this claim; ask me Python or R first."  |  为这个结论做一套投稿级图件，先问我用 Python 还是 R。
- "Use natsci-writing-zh. Turn this design document into a complete Nature Perspective with figures and a box."  |  把这份设计文档写成完整 Nature Perspective，带图和一个 Box。
- "Use natsci-writing. Verify every citation and the AI disclosure before I submit."  |  投稿前核对每条引文和 AI 披露。

## Verify  |  校验

~~~bash
python tools/validate_package.py
python tools/check_prose_style.py README.md README.zh-CN.md
python tools/validate_package.py --installed ~/.agents/skills
python tools/build_manifest.py
~~~

The validator checks frontmatter, names, versions, companion links, reference links, absolute paths, stale names, assets and package documents. It uses only the Python standard library.  |  校验器检查 frontmatter、名称、版本、companion、参考链接、绝对路径、旧名称、资产和包文档；只用 Python 标准库。文风检查器扫描模板对立、防御性表达和高频 AI 词。
