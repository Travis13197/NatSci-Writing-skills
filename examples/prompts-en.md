# Ready-to-use prompts (English)

## Complete manuscript

"Use the natsci-writing skill. Turn the attached data, figures and notes into a complete Nature Article: title, summary paragraph, introduction, results, discussion, methods, references, figure legends and statements. Follow the journal structure and mark every missing input."

## Section drafting

"Use natsci-writing. Draft the Results section from these analyses and figures. One subsection per claim, question → design → observation with statistics → inference; keep method detail in Methods."

## Complete figure set

"Use natsci-writing. Build a submission-grade figure set for this claim. First give the figure contract and panel map; ask me Python or R; then produce SVG/PDF/600-dpi PNG, per-panel exports if SEAL, captions and a release audit."

## Revision

"Use natsci-writing. Revise this manuscript for Nature style: preserve my terminology and data, repair reasoning before wording, and return tracked changes plus a change log."

## Formatting

"Use natsci-writing. Convert this manuscript to Nature format: structure, abstract limit, reference style, figure legends, statements, line numbers, and a compliance checklist. Do not change scientific meaning."

## Response letter

"Use natsci-writing. Draft the point-by-point response letter from these reviewer comments and my revision notes. Build the tracker, mark missing facts, and keep the tone evidence-forward and non-defensive."

## Pre-submission review

"Use natsci-writing. Review this manuscript as three Nature-style referees plus a cross-review synthesis, grounded only in the supplied material."

## Bilingual revision

"Use natsci-writing. Revise this English manuscript and provide a corresponding Chinese version, bolding every changed span in both languages."

## AI writing audit

"Use natsci-writing. Audit this manuscript against the target journal's AI policy and the AI-writing negative list: authorship, disclosure, citations, numbers, confidential material, third-party rights and fabrication risk. Return a pass/fix table, required disclosure text and residual questions."

## AI figure audit

"Use natsci-writing. Classify each figure as data, conceptual, graphical abstract or AI-assisted layout. For any AI-assisted figure, build the provenance record, check the target journal's image policy, and return disclosure text, QC findings and a negative-checklist result."
