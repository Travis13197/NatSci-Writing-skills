# AI use negative checklist

## Authorship and disclosure

- [ ] AI is not listed as author, contributor or corresponding contact.
- [ ] AI use is disclosed in the required place(s), with tool, task, sections and oversight.
- [ ] Disclosure is consistent across manuscript, figure captions, cover letter and submission system.
- [ ] No claim that "no AI was used" when it was.
- [ ] No AI paraphrasing to evade detection or hide assistance.

## Text and citations

- [ ] No fabricated references, DOIs, URLs, ISBNs, accessions or approvals.
- [ ] Every citation supports its sentence and has been verified against the source.
- [ ] No hallucinated methods, parameters, software versions, sample sizes or statistics.
- [ ] No text recycling, self-plagiarism, duplicate submission or salami slicing.
- [ ] No paper-mill patterns, tortured phrases or purchased authorship.
- [ ] No AI-generated peer-review reports or editorial decisions.
- [ ] No AI-generated cover letter or significance statement that overstates the paper.
- [ ] The manuscript reflects substantive human analysis, interpretation and verification.

## Data and images

- [ ] No AI-generated or AI-altered research images, microscopy, gels, blots, plots or structures.
- [ ] No generative fill, object removal or synthetic data in a research image.
- [ ] Every AI-assisted figure has a provenance record.
- [ ] Data figures were generated from real data and code.
- [ ] Conceptual AI figures are labelled conceptual and disclosed.
- [ ] No fake scale bars, error bars, significance marks or success rates.
- [ ] Prompt and outputs contain no confidential, patient or third-party restricted material.
- [ ] No copyrighted characters, logos, photos or identifiable people in prompts/outputs.
- [ ] Tool terms and publisher licence permit the generated content.

## Review and boundaries

- [ ] Reviewers/editors did not upload manuscripts or data to public AI tools.
- [ ] Journal policy URL and date recorded.
- [ ] Copyright, licence and tool terms checked.
- [ ] AI detection was not used as a substitute for human review.
- [ ] A human author can defend every sentence, number and figure.
