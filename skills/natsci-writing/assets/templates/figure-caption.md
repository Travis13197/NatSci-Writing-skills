## Checklist

- Title is a claim, not a label.
- Panel letters match the figure exactly.
- Evaluation unit defined before any percentage.
- Error bars defined (s.d., s.e.m., 95% CI or IQR).
- Test and correction stated once.
- Scale bars and structure identifiers included.
- No method inventory, no defensive boilerplate.
