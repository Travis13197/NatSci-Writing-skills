## How the system was built

The system was built end-to-end from the author's own research projects and long-term conversations with AI coding agents: the argument rules, section playbooks, figure evidence architecture, statistics checks, revision workflows and project figure standards come from real manuscripts, figures, reviews and revisions rather than a generic prompt collection. The NatSci corpus analysis added measured writing and figure statistics from 868 Nature/Science records (317 full texts, 1,504 parsed captions); the corpus itself is not redistributed.
