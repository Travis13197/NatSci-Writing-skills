# NatSci-Writing-skills v1.2.1

Publication-system upgrade after syncing the published GitHub version and auditing the latest sources.

## New in 1.2.1

- **AI use and integrity**: AI authorship ban, disclosure, permitted/prohibited uses, confidentiality, AI-tone control, journal policy table (Nature, Science, ICMJE, COPE, Elsevier, Springer Nature) and an audit.
- **Literature search and citation**: source hierarchy, search strings and screening record, PRISMA-style documentation, evidence synthesis, citation verification and RIS/BibTeX/Zotero management.
- **Proofreading and polish**: five passes (spelling/terms, grammar, inappropriate words, awkward expression, academic style), sentence-level rules, grounding/rhythm, number/reference consistency.
- **Perspective and Nature section craft**: intellectual centre, discovery sequence, accountability, formal content; abstract/Introduction/Results/Discussion moves and audits.
- **Reviewer response and data**: technical-concern taxonomy, severity, forensic consistency, package consistency; mandatory deposition, FAIR, RRIDs, reporting guidelines.
- **Templates**: AI disclosure, literature search plan, data/code/materials checklist (14 templates per language).
- **README** re-polished in English and Chinese, preserving the author-published version, with v1.2.1 notes, AI section and updated counts.
- Integrated independent skills (insight-led-scientific-perspective, literature-review, grammar-checker, polish, writing-beats) and latest nature-skills upstream (commit c6701db).
